🎙️ Sound Recorder

🏠 Back to the app

Record each word and each sound once, and the app plays your recordings on every device. Anything not recorded yet uses the device's own voice.

  1. On a computer, run python serve.py in the project folder and open localhost:8000/recorder.html. Recordings then save straight into audio/words/ and audio/sounds/.
  2. Not using serve.py? Tap Choose project folder once and pick the sight-words folder (Chrome or Edge), and it's remembered next time.
  3. Tap 🎙️, say the word or sound, then tap ⏹. Listen, and tap 💾 to keep it or 🎙️ to try again.
  4. Commit the new files in audio/ so every device gets them.

Sounds: keep them short and clean: "b", not "buh". Hold the ones that can be held, like "mmm" and "sss", for about a second.
