🎙 Sound Recorder

Record each word and each sound once, and the app plays your recordings on every device. Anything not recorded yet uses the device's own voice.

  1. Open this page on a computer, from the project folder (python -m http.server 8000, then localhost:8000/recorder.html).
  2. Tap Choose project folder and pick the sight-words folder, so recordings save straight into audio/.
  3. Tap 🎙, say the word or sound, then tap ⏹. Listen, and tap 💾 to keep it or 🎙 to try again.
  4. Commit the new files in audio/ so every device gets them.

Sounds: keep them short and clean: "b", not "buh". Hold the ones that can be held, like "mmm" and "sss", for about a second.
