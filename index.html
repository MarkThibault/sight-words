          👋 Start here

          1. Let your child play Let's Read! most days. A star means a word they can read on their own.
          2. Once a week, look at 🧡 Needs practice on the Progress page, and tap ▶ Practice these words to play them together.
          3. Now and then, Save a copy on the Settings page, so progress is never lost.
          4. Look through each page once: Words turns words on and off, and Settings has the voice, sound effects and calmer animations.

          The games

          • Let's Read! ▶ shows up to words, one at a time. Tap the word to hear it, or 🧩 Sound it out to hear it one sound at a time. When your child can read it, tap I know it! ⭐. Otherwise tap Next ➜. Once a word has been heard or sounded out, I know it! turns off for that word, so a star always means your child read it alone.
          • 🔁 Check-up is the same, with only words that already have a star: the ones not seen for the longest, so learned words don't fade. It opens once your child has a star. Here Next says Skip ➜: a word skipped in a Check-up keeps its star and sticker, and goes on 🧡 Needs practice for you.
          • ⏱️ Timed run goes through every word against the clock and saves the best time.
          • 👂 Listen & tap: hear a word, then find it among .
          • 🧱 Build the word: hear a word, then put its sounds in order. Each sound starts as a blank line. Tapping the word sounds out the parts placed so far and the next blank, as a hint.

          Both games are open from the start and use every word that's turned on, including words without a star yet, so they're good practice for new words too. In the games, a wrong answer gets a soft sound and another try, never a failure.

          The words are learned in a set order, starting with short, common ones like I, a and the. Every round and game (but Check-up) includes the next words your child doesn't have a star for yet, so they keep coming back until they're learned. They're under Learning next on the Progress page. The rest of the round mixes in other words, known and new. The Words page lists them all in that order.

          Stars and stickers

          Only I know it! earns a star, and every star comes with a sticker. New stickers fly into the 🐵 / 🙈 switch and wait there until your child opens it. The switch in the top-left corner opens your child's sticker corner: it hides the title, buttons and ⚙️, and brings out 🏆, 🎲 (tidies the stickers) and 🖼️. Stickers can only be dragged there, and the switch slides back to bring everything back. A red bubble on the switch counts what's waiting: new stickers, prizes to pick and new scenes. The other games are practice and never change stars or stickers. You can also give or take away a sticker on the Words page.

          Stickers come in themed sets of . The star pill at the bottom of the home screen shows the set being collected, and finishing it unlocks a new scene behind the stickers, like the beach or outer space. Once the first scene is earned, 🖼️ in the sticker corner switches between them.

          Medals and trophies

          Finishing a game of Listen & tap or Build the word earns a medal: bronze just for finishing, silver for % of the points, and gold for every word right the first time. A game left partway earns nothing. After two games of the same kind in a row, that game waits until your child plays the other game or a reading round, so medals come from a mix of play.

          Each medal fills the trophy pill at the bottom of the home screen: 1 for bronze, 2 for silver and 3 for gold. Every earns a trophy, and each trophy lets your child pick a prize sticker for the board, with a 🏆 tag instead of a word. Tap the trophy pill (or 🏆 in the sticker corner) to see them all, or to pick a prize saved for later.

          ⚙️ Settings

          The Settings page chooses the voice, turns the little sound effects off (words are still spoken), and turns on Calmer animations, so stickers and medals appear without flying and there's no confetti. Calmer animations is always on when the device itself is set to reduce motion. These stay as they are after Reset all progress.

          ⬇️ Saving or moving progress

          Progress is saved only on this device, and removing the app erases it. On the Settings page, Save a copy saves it to a small file, usually in Downloads. Keep it somewhere safe, or open it on another device with Load a copy, which asks before replacing that device's progress. The voice choice isn't in the copy, since each device has its own voices.

          📅 This week

          On the Progress page, This week shows the days your child played a round or a game, Sunday to Saturday, and the words they got a star for that week. Stars you give on the Words page aren't counted. The ‹ arrow shows last week. It starts counting days from this version of the app.

          🧡 Needs practice

          The Progress page lists up to words your child finds hard, the most missed first, and the Words page marks every one with 🧡. Your child never sees it, and it never changes stars, stickers or medals. A word is on the list when:

          • it was missed times in its last tries in the games: a wrong tap in Listen & tap, which also notes the word tapped instead, or a wrong tile in Build the word. This counts words with a star too, since I know it! is your child's own tap.
          • or it has no star yet and was passed with Next in reading rounds.
          • or it has a star and was passed with Skip in a Check-up. It keeps its star and sticker, and comes off the list when your child taps I know it! on it again.

          ▶ Practice these words closes this corner and asks you to hand the device to your child, who taps Let's read! to start a reading round of just those words. It's a round like any other: I know it! still earns a star. Below the list, a tip suggests a way to practice them away from the screen, a new one each day.

          Getting it right the first time times in a row in the games takes it off, and a star clears the reading rounds. It starts counting from this version of the app, so the list fills up over a few days of play. Reset all progress clears it.

          Turning words on and off

          Each word on the Words page has a switch. A word that's off is skipped in every round and game, and its sticker is hidden until it's back on. Words stay off even after Reset all progress, and at least one word always stays on.

          Installing for offline play

          • iPad / iPhone: open the site in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
          • Android: open the site in Chrome, then ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).

          Open it once while online so everything is saved. When a new version is ready, an Update button shows at the bottom of the home screen.

          Progress is saved on each device. On iPad and iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own progress apart from Safari, so it starts fresh.

          Privacy

          Sight Words collects nothing: progress stays on this device and the app sends nothing anywhere. A copy you save goes only where you put it. Read the privacy page.
