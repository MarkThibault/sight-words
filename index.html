      📖 What's in this app

      The games

      • Let's Read! ▶ shows up to words, one at a time. Tap the word to hear it, or 🧩 Sound it out to hear it one sound at a time. When your child can read it, tap I know it! ⭐. Otherwise tap Next ➜.
      • 🎯 Missing words is the same, with only the words that don't have a star yet.
      • ⏱️ Timed run goes through every word against the clock and saves the best time.
      • 👂 Listen & tap: hear a word, then find it among .
      • 🧱 Build the word: hear a word, then put its sounds in order.

      Both games are open from the start and use every word that's turned on, including words without a star yet, so they're good practice for new words too. In the games, a wrong answer gets a soft sound and another try, never a failure.

      The words are learned in a set order, starting with short, common ones like I, a and the. Every round and game includes the next words your child doesn't have a star for yet, so they keep coming back until they're learned. The rest of the round mixes in other words, known and new. The word list in the Grown-up Corner is in that order.

      Stars and stickers

      Only I know it! earns a star, and every star comes with a sticker. New stickers fly into the 🐵 / 🙈 switch and wait there until your child opens it. The switch in the top-left corner opens your child's sticker corner: it hides the title, buttons and ⚙️, and brings out 🏆, 🎲 (tidies the stickers) and 🖼️. Stickers can only be dragged there, and the switch slides back to bring everything back. A red bubble on the switch counts what's waiting: new stickers, prizes to pick and new scenes. The other games are practice and never change stars or stickers. You can also give or take away a sticker here in the Grown-up Corner.

      Stickers come in themed sets of . The star pill at the bottom of the home screen shows the set being collected, and finishing it unlocks a new scene behind the stickers, like the beach or outer space. Once the first scene is earned, 🖼️ in the sticker corner switches between them.

      Medals and trophies

      Finishing a game of Listen & tap or Build the word earns a medal: bronze just for finishing, silver for % of the points, and gold for every word right the first time. A game left partway earns nothing. After two games of the same kind in a row, that game waits until your child plays the other game or a reading round, so medals come from a mix of play.

      Each medal fills the trophy pill at the bottom of the home screen: 1 for bronze, 2 for silver and 3 for gold. Every earns a trophy, and each trophy lets your child pick a prize sticker for the board, with a 🏆 tag instead of a word. Tap the trophy pill (or 🏆 in the sticker corner) to see them all, or to pick a prize saved for later.

      Turning words on and off

      Each word has a switch. A word that's off is skipped in every round and game, and its sticker is hidden until it's back on. Words stay off even after Reset all progress, and at least one word always stays on.

      Installing for offline play

      • iPad / iPhone: open the site in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
      • Android: open the site in Chrome, then ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).

      Open it once while online so everything is saved. When a new version is ready, an Update button shows at the bottom of the home screen.

      Progress is saved on each device. On iPad and iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own progress apart from Safari, so it starts fresh.

      Privacy

      Sight Words collects nothing: progress stays on this device and the app sends nothing anywhere. Read the privacy page.

      ⚠️

      Erase all progress?

      This erases all stars, stickers, best times, medals and trophies on this device. Turned-off words stay off.
