      📖 What's in this app

      The games

      • Let's Read! ▶ shows up to words, one at a time. Tap the word to hear it, or 🧩 Sound it out to hear it one sound at a time. When your child can read it, tap I know it! ⭐. Otherwise tap Next ➜.
      • 🎯 Missing words is the same, with only the words that don't have a star yet.
      • ⏱ Timed run goes through every word against the clock and saves the best time.
      • 👂 Listen & tap: hear a word, then find it among . It opens once words have stars.
      • 🧱 Build the word: hear a word, then put its sounds in order. It opens once words have stars.

      In the games, a wrong answer gets a soft sound and another try, never a failure.

      Stars and stickers

      Only I know it! earns a star, and every star comes with a surprise sticker on the home screen. Stickers can be dragged anywhere, and 🎲 tidies them up. The other games are practice and never change stars or stickers. You can also give or take away a sticker here in the Grown-up Corner.

      Medals and trophies

      Finishing a game of Listen & tap or Build the word earns a medal: bronze just for finishing, silver for % of the points, and gold for every word right the first time. A game left partway earns nothing.

      Each medal fills the trophy meter at the bottom of the home screen: 1 for bronze, 2 for silver and 3 for gold. Every earns a trophy. Tap 🏆 to see them all.

      Turning words on and off

      Each word has a switch. A word that's off is skipped in every round and game, left out of the star count, and its sticker is hidden until it's back on. Words stay off even after Reset all progress, and at least one word always stays on.

      Installing for offline play

      • iPad / iPhone: open the site in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
      • Android: open the site in Chrome, then ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).

      Open it once while online so everything is saved. When a new version is ready, an Update button shows at the bottom of the home screen.

      Progress is saved on each device. On iPad and iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own progress apart from Safari, so it starts fresh.

      ⚠️

      Erase all progress?

      This erases all stars, stickers, best times, medals and trophies on this device. Turned-off words stay off.
