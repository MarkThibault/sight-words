🔒 Privacy

Sight Words · last updated 8 November 2026

Sight Words collects nothing. Your child's progress stays on your device, and the app sends nothing anywhere.

What the app saves

To remember your child's progress, the app saves it in the browser's storage on the device: stars, stickers and where they sit on the board, scenes, best times, medals, trophies, prize stickers, and the grown-up settings (words turned off, the voice chosen, and whether the tip about ⚙️ has been seen). None of it has your child's name or anything else about who they are.

This stays on the device. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't shared between devices. When the app is installed, it asks the browser to keep this saved progress so it isn't cleared when the device runs low on space.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No microphone, camera, location or contacts.
  • No links out of the app for your child to find. The only one is the link to this page, inside the grown-up guide.

Where the app comes from

The app's files, including its font and recorded words, all come from the app's own web address. It doesn't load anything from any other site. Those files are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard records of requests (such as IP addresses) for security. The app sends GitHub nothing about your child. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The device's voice

Words and sounds play from recordings that come with the app. Only if a recording is missing does the app use the device's own text-to-speech voice to say the word, and some of those voices work online through the device or browser maker.

Children

Sight Words is made for young children learning to read. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Erasing progress

  • Reset all progress in the Grown-up Corner (press and hold ⚙️) erases stars, stickers, best times, medals and trophies. Turned-off words stay off.
  • Clearing the browser's data for this site, or uninstalling the app and clearing that data, erases everything.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top. Questions are welcome on the app's GitHub page.
